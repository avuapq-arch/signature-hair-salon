Questions

Where is Signature Hair Salon?

We are at 7 South Clerk Street, Edinburgh EH8 9JD, in Newington on the Southside, a short walk from the University of Edinburgh's central campus.

How much is a men's haircut?

A men's haircut is £20. A skin fade is £23, a zero fade is £21 and a men's long haircut is £30. Clippers-only all-over cuts are £13.

Do you offer student prices?

Yes. A men's student haircut is £18, compared with £20 for a standard men's haircut. A women's student short haircut is £22, compared with £25. Kids under 10 pay £15 and customers over 65 pay £16.

Is there a barber near the University of Edinburgh with a student discount?

Yes. We are at 7 South Clerk Street in Newington, a short walk from the University of Edinburgh's central campus. A men's student haircut is £18 and a women's student short haircut is £22.

Do you do beard trims and hot towel shaves?

Yes. A beard trim and line up is £13, an Italian beard is £23, and a hot shave with hot towel is £23.

Do you cut women's hair?

Yes. A women's short haircut is £25, or £22 for students.

How do I book?

Book online 24/7 at signaturebarber3jpr.setmore.com or call 0131 273 8355. Walk-ins are welcome too.

Can I walk in without booking?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, or you can book online 24/7.

What if I'm not happy with my haircut?

If you're not 100% happy, we'll fix it free within 5 days.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available in the shop.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. We accept cash, credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. We are closed on Sunday and Monday.
