Questions

Where is Signature Hair Salon?

We are at 7 South Clerk Street, Edinburgh EH8 9JD, in Newington on the Southside, a short walk from the University of Edinburgh's central campus.

How much is a men's haircut?

A men's haircut is £17. A skin fade is £20, a zero fade is £18 and a men's long haircut is £25. Clippers-only all-over cuts are £13.

Do you offer student prices?

Yes. A student haircut is £15. Kids under 10 and customers over 65 pay £14.

Do you do beard trims and hot towel shaves?

Yes. A beard trim is £10, a beard trim and line up is £12, and a hot shave with hot towel is £20.

How do I book?

Book online at signaturebarber3jpr.setmore.com or call 0131 273 8355.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. We are closed on Sunday and Monday.
